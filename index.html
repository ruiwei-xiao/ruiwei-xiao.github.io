About

Ruiwei Xiao is a PhD student at CMU HCII and co-founder of ActiveAI. Her research and entrepreneurship in AI for Education and AI Literacy have advanced culturally-responsive learning and responsible use of AI in multicultural and multilingual environments, impacting over 50,000 learners worldwide.

She is also the lead organizer of the ALIT4ALL workshop, the largest AI Literacy workshop bringing together hundreds of researchers and practitioners internationally. Her work has been supported by Gates Foundation and CMU seed grants; her startup, ActiveAI, has been recognized through the NSF I-Corps Regional AI/Robotic Cohort and developed in partnership with Microsoft, Google Data Center, Junyi Academy, and endorsed by multiple city mayors.
